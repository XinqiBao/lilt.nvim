# The Tyger

William Blake (1757–1827), “The Tyger,” first published in *Songs of
Experience* (1794).

Text source: Wikisource's [validated transcription of an engraved copy at the
British Museum](https://en.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=Songs_of_Innocence_and_of_Experience_(1826)/Songs_of_Experience/The_Tyger&oldid=16305701),
in *Songs of Innocence and of Experience* (1826). The heading is omitted;
verse lines, punctuation, capitalization, and the spelling “sieze” follow that
transcription. Stanzas are separated by blank physical rows. This is Blake's
engraved text, whose punctuation differs from many later editions.

The poem and this historical edition are in the public domain in the United
States and in countries with a life-plus-70-year copyright term. No modern
translation, editorial notes, or illustrations are included. The plugin's MIT
code license does not relicense the literary text.
